About Me

I'm Anirudh Kurgund — a builder at the early stage of what I intend to be a long journey. I don't specialise in one thing yet, and I think that's a strength. I move across ideas, connect dots between them, and find the most interesting problems to work on.

My first real project, NexLedger, came from a personal frustration with how people track money — so I built something that does it quietly, privately, and without needing the internet. That instinct — notice a problem, build a solution — is how I approach most things.

I'm currently learning, shipping, and figuring out what I'm capable of. The goal is simple: build things that matter, keep getting better, and go far.
